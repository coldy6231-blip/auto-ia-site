Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 12, 2026

AUTO IA runs on your computer. The authorizations for connected accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X), your videos and your settings are stored only on that computer. You can delete everything at any time, without depending on us.

1. Disconnect an account in AUTO IA

  1. Open AUTO IA and go to Canais & Contas (Channels & Accounts).
  2. In the “Contas conectadas” (Connected accounts) list, click the ✕ next to the account.
  3. Confirm. The authorization for that account is deleted from the computer and its scheduled posts are cancelled.

2. Revoke access on the platform itself

  • Instagram: in the app, Settings and activity → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Active → AUTO IA → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions → AUTO IA → Remove access.
  • YouTube (Google): myaccount.google.com/permissions → AUTO IA → Remove access.
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → AUTO IA → Revoke.

3. Delete all software data

Uninstall AUTO IA and delete its data folder (the data folder inside the program folder). This removes videos, settings and all authorizations.

4. Data we hold

We only keep contact details, license purchase data and support messages. To request deletion, write to coldy6231@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. We confirm receipt and complete the request within 15 days. Data that tax law requires us to keep is retained only for the legal period.
