Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Versão em português

This Policy explains how AUTO IA, provided by Gustavo Almeida ("we"), handles personal data, in line with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018 — LGPD).

1. Summary

  • AUTO IA runs on the user's computer. Videos, settings and the authorizations of connected accounts are stored on that computer.
  • We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.
  • This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

2. Data from connected accounts

When a user connects an account, the platform gives AUTO IA an authorization (token) and some basic profile data. AUTO IA uses this data only to show which account is connected and to publish the videos the user approves.

PlatformData receivedPurpose
TikTokAccount identifier (open_id), display name and avatar (user.info.basic scope); the account's posting information, such as available privacy options and maximum video duration; permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload and video.publish scopes).Show the connected account, respect the account's privacy options, and publish or send as a draft the videos the user approved.
YouTube (Google)Channel identifier and name; permission to upload videos and read statistics of uploaded videos.Publish approved videos and show views of videos published with AUTO IA.
Instagram (Meta)Professional account identifier, username and account type (instagram_business_basic permission); permission to publish content (instagram_business_content_publish) and to read post insights (instagram_business_manage_insights).Show the connected account, publish the Reels and Stories the user approved, and show their performance. We do not read messages, comments or followers.
XUser identifier and username; permission to post.Publish approved videos.

AUTO IA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data received from Meta is used in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms. Data from connected accounts is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties.

3. Created content

Scripts, voice-overs, images and videos are generated and stored on the user's computer. To create them, AUTO IA sends text, audio or images to the AI and stock media services the user configures (for example Google Gemini, Groq, ElevenLabs, Cloudflare and Pixabay), using the user's own keys. Those transfers follow those services' privacy policies.

4. Data we receive

We only receive what the user sends us directly: contact and purchase data for the license (name, email, and payment data processed by the payment provider) and support messages. We use this data to sell the license, issue tax documents and provide support.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with: the platforms the user connects (to publish), the services the user configures (to create content), the payment provider (for the purchase), and authorities when required by law.

6. Retention

Data on the user's computer is kept until the user deletes it. Purchase data is kept for the period required by tax law; support messages for up to 2 years.

7. Deleting data and revoking access

  • Disconnect an account: remove it on AUTO IA's "Channels & Accounts" screen; the authorization is deleted from the computer.
  • Revoke on the platform: on TikTok, Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services; on Google, myaccount.google.com/permissions; on Instagram/Facebook, Settings → Business integrations.
  • Delete everything: uninstall AUTO IA and delete its data folder.
  • Data we hold: request deletion at coldy6231@gmail.com; we reply within 15 days.

Full step-by-step instructions are on the Data Deletion page.

8. Your rights (LGPD)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion and information about sharing, and withdraw consent, by writing to coldy6231@gmail.com. You may also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

9. Security

Account authorizations stay on the user's computer and are only sent to the platforms themselves over secure connections (HTTPS). Users should protect access to their own computer.

10. Minimum age

AUTO IA is intended for users aged 18 or over.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy; the date is shown at the top of the page. Data controller: Gustavo Almeida — coldy6231@gmail.com.
