How it works

  1. Install it on your computer. AUTO IA runs locally: your videos and settings stay on your machine.
  2. Connect your accounts. Sign in with each platform's official login (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X) and authorize AUTO IA to post for you. You can disconnect at any time.
  3. Create videos. Pick a topic or let the AI suggest one; AUTO IA prepares script, voice, scenes, captions and cover.
  4. Review and approve. Watch the video, edit the text for each platform, choose who can view it and the interaction settings. Nothing is posted without your confirmation.
  5. Publish on schedule. AUTO IA sends the approved video to the chosen account at the scheduled time and shows the result.

Posting to TikTok

The TikTok integration uses the official Content Posting API. Before each post, AUTO IA shows the connected account, a preview of the video and an editable caption, and asks you to choose the privacy level and whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch. AI-made videos can be labeled as AI-generated content. By posting, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

Posting to Instagram

The Instagram integration uses Meta's official API (Instagram API with Instagram Login). You sign in with your professional account on Instagram's own login screen and authorize AUTO IA. The software shows the name of the connected account, publishes the Reels and Stories you approved, and displays performance numbers for those posts. You can remove the account at any time; see how to delete your data.

Frequently asked questions

Does AUTO IA post without my review?

No. Every video waits on an approval screen until you confirm. You choose the account, the text, the privacy level and the time.

Where are my videos and passwords kept?

On your computer. AUTO IA never asks for or stores your social media passwords: you connect through each platform's official login screen, which only hands over an authorization you can revoke.

Can I use my own videos?

Yes, as long as you have the rights to publish them.

Are AI-made videos labeled?

Yes. Where the platform offers the option, AUTO IA marks the video as AI-generated content.

How do I disconnect an account or delete my data?

Remove the account on the “Canais & Contas” (Channels & Accounts) screen or revoke access on the platform itself. Step-by-step instructions are on the Data Deletion page.
